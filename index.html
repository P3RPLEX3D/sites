Key Principles

The Philosophy: Making 30lbs Heavy

Since we can't increase the weight, our focus shifts to *how* we lift it. This is done through controlled tempo (3-1-2-1), high reps (10-20), short rest (60-90s), and a strong mind-muscle connection.

How to Progress

Continuously challenge your muscles by: 1) Adding more reps per set. 2) Adding an extra set. 3) Decreasing rest time. 4) Incorporating pauses at the hardest part of the lift.

Cool-Down & Recovery

After each workout, stretch the muscles you worked. Consume a protein-rich meal within 1-2 hours, and aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep for optimal muscle repair.
